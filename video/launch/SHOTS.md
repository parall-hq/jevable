# Shot list

128 BPM, D minor, 16 bars = 30 s, after a 0.5 s hold of key art. A beat is
0.469 s, a bar 1.875 s. Exact times live in [`src/cues.ts`](src/cues.ts);
this is the intent behind them.

**The world.** A dark control room lit only by amber. The agent is a Claude
Code session, drawn in jevable's own style (the name as text, no marks), its
status light the amber dot. Asleep, it waits at the prompt; each wake is a
`Monitor` event in its log, a new turn ("Thinking…"), and "+$0.12" flying to
a meter (the wakes were priced on Claude Code). Events are real lines from
the repo, on cards. Depth comes from the site's dot grid laid down as a
floor, cards passing close to the lens out of focus, and the light the
session throws when it wakes.

**The analogy: a sorting line.** A belt carries each line toward the agent. A
gate decides what gets through; what gets through wakes the agent and costs
a turn; what does not drops off the belt. The shots swap the gate: none, a
stencil that fits one word (grep), a reader with a needle gauge (Jev).

**One watch throughout.** Shots 2 and 4 use the same posts (@thsottiaux's,
from the site's demo) and the same question, so the pun that fools grep comes
back and is read correctly. Shot 2 also flips to the status-page alert to
show grep missing real outages worded without its word.

**Score.** Bar 1: clock ticks and a sub drone; every wake is a pluck, so the
flood is also the riser, ending in a tape stop on the freeze. Bars 4–5
half-time, a metal clank on the sign, a sour buzz for the needless wake. Bar
6 drops on the dot. Bars 8–10: each needle is a pitched glide, each wake a
bell and a coin. Bar 11 breaks down under the sweep, bar 12 rises, bar 13
slams on the stamp. Bars 15–16 resolve to F with a last bell on the dot.
